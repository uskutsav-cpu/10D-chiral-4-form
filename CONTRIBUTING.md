# Contributing

Research changes should be small, auditable, and tied to a claim or falsification target.

## Pull-request checklist
- What scientific claim changes?
- What exact artifact/certificate supports it?
- What negative control could falsify it?
- Does the change alter conventions, basis order, or provenance?
- Are imported results still clearly distinguished from independently reproduced ones?
- Does any wording accidentally conflate static span, forcing span, seed closure, or generalized-generator closure?

Do not merge a headline numerical change without a machine-readable artifact and a test guarding the interpretation.
