# Theory and Definitions

This document fixes terminology so that distinct notions of “generated by the stress tensor” are never conflated.

## 1. Graded invariant space

Let
\[
A=\bigoplus_d A_d
\]
be the graded space of Lorentz-invariant scalar polynomials in the self-dual five-form, with `d` denoting five-form degree.

A chosen verified atlas supplies explicit coordinates on each `A_d` through the trusted cutoff.

## 2. Static stress algebra

At the free theory, construct the mixed stress matrix `tau` (normalization pinned separately). The **static stress subspace** in degree `d` is the span of degree-`d` scalar polynomials formed from characteristic stress invariants such as
\[
\operatorname{Tr}(\tau^k)
\]
and products thereof.

This is a useful baseline but it is **not** the nonlinear reachable space.

## 3. Dynamic reachability

For an interaction \(\mathcal V\), the stress tensor itself changes. A scalar stress flow has the form
\[
\partial_\lambda\mathcal V
=f\!\left(p_1(T[\mathcal V]),\ldots,p_m(T[\mathcal V]);\lambda\right),
\]
for a declared class of scalar generators `p_i`.

Fixing a seed \(\mathcal V(0)\), iterative formal expansion generates a **reachable subspace** `R_d` in each degree.

The key principle is:

> nonlinear feedback can make `R_d` much larger than the static stress span.

## 4. Obstruction quotient

Define
\[
Q_d=A_d/R_d.
\]

A graph-basis complement is merely one coordinate representation of `Q_d`. Intrinsic information should be expressed via:
- quotient classes;
- annihilators `R_d^perp` in the dual space;
- tensorial/representation-theoretic structures whose classes span the quotient.

## 5. Seed augmentation versus generator augmentation

These are mathematically different experiments.

### Seed augmentation
Start with
\[
\mathcal V(0)=\mathcal V_{free}+cS.
\]
The flow law may still depend only on stress invariants.

### Generator augmentation
Change the differential law itself:
\[
\partial_\lambda\mathcal V=f(T,S;\lambda).
\]

A result about the former cannot be advertised as a theorem about the latter.

## 6. Minimal generalized completion

For candidate intrinsic generators `S = {S_1,...,S_r}`, define the generalized reachable space `R_d(T,S)`.

A completion is **cardinality-minimal in the declared generator class** if:
1. it attains the target closure through the declared degree; and
2. no generator set of smaller cardinality in that class does so.

Removal-minimality of one displayed set is weaker than global cardinality minimality and must be labelled accordingly.

## 7. Nonanalytic/conformal localization

Polynomial results live in a polynomial invariant algebra. Models involving `sqrt(I4)`, fractional powers, or invariant ratios require an algebraic/localized extension, for example by adjoining `r` with
\[
r^2=I_4
\]
on a generic patch.

No polynomial reachability theorem is automatically global in this enlarged space.

## 8. Certificate philosophy

The computation should output both a result and a falsifiable witness:
- rank claim -> pivot/minor or exact row-reduction witness;
- non-membership -> annihilator with nonzero evaluation;
- basis-independent quotient -> dual certificate invariant under coordinate changes;
- minimality -> explicit successful set plus smaller-set/removal failures;
- rational lift -> reconstruction bound plus held-out-prime validation.
