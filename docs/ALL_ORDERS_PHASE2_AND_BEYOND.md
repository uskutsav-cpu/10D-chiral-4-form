Phase 1 proved:

- field-degree filtration / truncation commutation;
- all-orders preservation of \(K_6=0\);

This phase codes every remaining theorem gate without overstating what the

## Degree 10

The frozen degree-10 obstruction certificate defines twelve independent
relations on top of the six exact lower-degree constraints. The new code
reconstructs the resulting 18-generator ideal exactly over \(\mathbb Q\),
solves it as a six-parameter graph, and checks exact tangency of every loaded
degree-10 vector field.

However, the frozen degree-10 model itself is labelled

`bounded_rational_candidate_with_unused_prime_validation_not_symbolic_physics_proof`.

Therefore the all-orders lift is presently **conditional on that physics/basis
map**. This is not a defect in the ideal algebra; it is the remaining map-proof
gate.

## Degree 12

The degree-12 project proves an exact characteristic-zero local theorem and
global constructive reachability, but the certificate does not yet expose a
compact exact polynomial orbit ideal together with exact ideal tangency.

Rank/nullity data are not silently treated as an ideal. The degree-12
all-orders promotion therefore remains blocked until those explicit generators
are derived.

## Abstract all-orders generalized completion

For a finite-dimensional representation of a reductive group in characteristic
zero, the polynomial invariant ring is finitely generated. If a homogeneous
Hilbert basis \(S_1,\ldots,S_m\) is admitted as genuine independent scalar
arguments in the generalized flow and arbitrary polynomial \(f(S)\) is
allowed, then every polynomial invariant interaction is directly reachable
from the free seed.

This proves existence of a finite all-orders generalized completion, but not an
explicit minimal D=10 Hilbert basis.
