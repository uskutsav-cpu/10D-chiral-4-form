# Degree-eight reduced orbit: derivation and constructive completion

## Precise scope

This is an exact rational calculation in the degree-eight truncation of the
declared analytic, derivative-free HLS stress-flow class, with signed
independent scalar controls. The physics-to-basis map has a bounded-residual
certificate conditional on the external invariant-count and convention
inputs listed separately. This document does not establish priority or a
nonanalytic/all-orders theorem.

Let `(a,j,k,b1,b2,b3,b4,b5,b6,p)` multiply
`(I4_1,I6_1,I6_2,I8_1,I8_2,I8_3,I8_4,I8_5,I8_6,I4_1^2)`.
Use `tau=48T`; the paper's quartic is `Tr M^2=2 I4_1`.

## Full generator vector fields

There are seven eligible trace monomials through degree eight. Nonzero
components of their vector fields are:

| Generator | Coefficient derivatives |
|---|---|
| `Tr tau` | `a'=20a; j'=40j; k'=40k; b_i'=60b_i; p'=60p` |
| `(Tr tau)^2` | `p'=400a^2` |
| `(Tr tau)(Tr tau^2)` | `p'=40a` |
| `Tr tau^2` | `a'=2; b1'=16a^2; b2'=-96a^2; p'=-8a^2` |
| `(Tr tau^2)^2` | `p'=4` |
| `Tr tau^3` | `j'=32/3; p'=12a` |
| `Tr tau^4` | `b1'=1` |

These are whole vector fields: the appearances of `a` and `a^2` cannot be
assigned independent control coefficients coordinate by coordinate.

## Obstruction and necessity

Set `Omega=b2+16a^3`. Direct differentiation gives

```
X_tr1(Omega)=60 Omega,
X_g(Omega)=0 for all six other generators.
```

Likewise `k,b3,b4,b5,b6` have homogeneous transport and no forcing. Hence the
free-seed orbit lies in

```
k=b3=b4=b5=b6=0,  b2=-16a^3.
```

The ideal generated by these six polynomials is tangent to every generator.
`ideals.py` checks this over QQ by Groebner reduction. Basis-change regressions
verify the statement after nonsingular homogeneous coupling transformations.

## Sufficiency by explicit controls

For arbitrary real/rational targets `(a,j,b1,p)=(A,J,B,P)`, use four signed
control amounts, starting at the free seed:

1. `Tr tau^2` for `A/2` gives
   `a=A, b1=8A^3/3, b2=-16A^3, p=-4A^3/3`.
2. `Tr tau^3` for `3J/32` gives `j=J` and adds `9AJ/8` to p.
3. `Tr tau^4` for `B-8A^3/3` sets b1=B.
4. `(Tr tau^2)^2` for `(P+4A^3/3-9AJ/8)/4` sets p=P.

Thus every point of the constraint graph is reachable in this finite model.
The graph is smooth with dimension four. The four-control minor on
`(a,j,b1,p)` is `256/3`. In contrast, its minimal linear invariant hull has
five dimensions. Its degree-eight projection spans three linear directions;
that observation does not mean three independent degree-eight controls.

## Genuine completion within a finite catalogue

Allow the six additional homogeneous scalar generators

```
I6_2, I8_2, I8_3, I8_4, I8_5, I8_6.
```

Their smallest mixed product with a stress scalar has degree at least ten,
so their only new contributions through degree eight are constant translation
fields in the corresponding coupling directions. Combined with the four base
controls above, they have an everywhere-nonzero ten-by-ten determinant.

Each is necessary in **this catalogue**. Omitting `I6_2` preserves `k=0`;
omitting one of `I8_3..I8_6` preserves its corresponding zero coefficient;
omitting `I8_2` preserves Omega=0. Every proper subset therefore fails to
fill the ten-dimensional model. The older five-generator proposal omitting
`I8_2` has only nine-dimensional reachability here, despite a larger linear
hull. This is a generator result, not a seed-augmentation result.

No assertion is made that six is minimal among arbitrary homogeneous or
inhomogeneous additional scalars. Changing the admissible catalogue is a new
optimization problem. No claim about physical admissibility follows from
full coefficient-space rank.
