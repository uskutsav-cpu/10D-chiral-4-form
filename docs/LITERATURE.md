# Literature and Novelty Boundary

## Closest primary literature

### Hutomo, Lechner, Sorokin — nonlinear chiral 4-forms in D=10
`arXiv:2509.14351`, JHEP 02 (2026) 147.

Relevant established results include:
- 81 generic functional invariants for a self-dual five-form modulo Lorentz transformations;
- stress-tensor invariant structures form only a restricted sector of the full invariant theory;
- generic D=10 nonlinear chiral-four-form theories require structures beyond stress invariants;
- the paper explicitly motivates the open question of whether subclasses exist that are generated exclusively by stress-tensor flows.

**Novelty boundary:** this repository must not claim the qualitative non-universality itself as new. The target is the **quantitative and structural reachability classification** and its intrinsic obstructions.

### Cederwall, Hutomo, Kuzenko, Lechner, Sorokin — invariants
`arXiv:2509.14350`, J. Phys. A 59 (2026) 065203.

Provides the representation/invariant-theory backdrop and low-order explicit structures. It is a primary reference for intrinsic tensor representatives beyond traces of `M`.

### Ferko et al. — interacting chiral forms and TTbar-like flows
`arXiv:2402.06947` (2024).

Develops the higher-dimensional chiral-form stress-flow program and the lower-dimensional universality baseline against which D=10 is exceptional.

### Brizio, Kade, Sfondrini, Sorokin — higher-dimensional TTbar-like deformations
`arXiv:2602.24058` (2026).

Develops higher-dimensional stress-flow structures for DNG/Born–Infeld/DBI-type systems. It does not, in the literature review performed for this project, provide the D=10 self-dual-five-form reachability/obstruction classification targeted here.

### Babaei-Aghbolagh, Chen, He, Hou — exact relevant stress flows and causality no-go
`arXiv:2606.13064` (2026).

Four-dimensional self-dual electrodynamics; relevant as a methodological precedent for structural no-go/selection results, not as a solution of the D=10 chiral-four-form problem.

## Project novelty target

The desired paper should contribute at least three things not supplied by the sources above:

1. **Dynamic reachability rather than static counting.** Quantify the nonlinear closure generated by the interacting stress tensor.
2. **Intrinsic obstruction classes.** Convert missing directions into basis-independent quotient/annihilator/tensor statements.
3. **Constructive minimal completion.** Determine the smallest additional genuine flow generators needed to restore closure in a declared class.

The strongest extension is a conformal/ModMax classification and/or an all-orders obstruction module.
