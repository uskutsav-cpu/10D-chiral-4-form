# All-orders obstruction program

## The filtration theorem

Write
\[
V=\sum_{d\ge4,\ d\ {\rm even}}V_d.
\]
In the declared HLS normalization,
\[
\tau=M-25B(DV,DV)+{\bf 1}\sum_d(d-2)V_d.
\]
For homogeneous terms,
\[
\deg(DV_d)=d-1,\qquad \deg B(DV_r,DV_s)=r+s-2.
\]
Since interactions begin at degree four, no term \(V_d\) with \(d>N\) can
contribute to the degree-\(\le N\) stress tensor. Products and traces only add
field degrees. Thus
\[
\boxed{\pi_{\le N}X_g(V)=X_g^{(\le N)}(\pi_{\le N}V)}.
\]

## Finite-jet lifting theorem

If an ideal \(J_N\) is exactly invariant under every truncated generator,
then its cylinder pullback is invariant under the full formal flow:
\[
X_g(\pi_{\le N}^{*}J_N)\subseteq \pi_{\le N}^{*}J_N.
\]

## Immediate all-orders K6 theorem

The exact reduced system has
\[
X_{\mathrm{tr1}}(K_6)=40K_6,\qquad X_g(K_6)=0
\]
for the other degree-six generators, so
\[
\dot K_6=40u_{\mathrm{tr1}}(\lambda)K_6.
\]
Therefore \(K_6=0\) is preserved by every formal analytic pure-stress flow.
The free seed lies on \(K_6=0\), giving an all-orders non-universality
obstruction.

## Degree-eight lifted ideal

The exact degree-eight ideal is generated by
\[
c_{I6_2},\ c_{I8_3},c_{I8_4},c_{I8_5},c_{I8_6},\
\Omega_8=c_{I8_2}+16c_{I4_1}^3.
\]
The reduced model verifies exact ideal tangency over \(\mathbb Q\), including
\[
\dot\Omega_8=60u_{\mathrm{tr1}}\Omega_8.
\]
Hence this whole finite ideal lifts to an all-orders invariant cylinder.
