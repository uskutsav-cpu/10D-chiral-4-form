# Draft result statement for review

Under the specified HLS normalization, homogeneous graph basis and analytic
scalar-control class, the degree-eight coefficient model has a free-seed
reachable set characterized by six polynomial equations: the sextic second
coefficient and four octic coefficients vanish, while the remaining octic
coefficient satisfies `b2=-16a^3`. A four-segment control construction reaches
every point of this four-dimensional graph. Its smallest invariant linear
hull is five-dimensional, illustrating why coefficient-span saturation is
not an orbit-classification theorem.

Within an explicitly declared catalogue of six additional homogeneous
scalars, each scalar is necessary for full local rank of the ten-coordinate
model. Sufficiency follows from a nonzero control determinant; necessity
follows from a surviving invariant constraint when each scalar is omitted.

The mapping to tensor physics is supported by an auditable bounded-residual
certificate using fixed small integer tensors, seven primes, and external
homogeneous invariant-dimension upper bounds. The result remains conditional
on those physics/convention and invariant-theory inputs. No statement about
priority, all orders, conformal reachability, or physical admissibility is
made here.
