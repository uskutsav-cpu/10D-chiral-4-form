\begin{abstract}
We organize an explicit Lorentz-invariant atlas for a real self-dual five-form
and a finite-order classification of polynomial stress-tensor flows into a
single reproducible research artifact. The atlas supplies 81 algebraically
independent contractions, an exact Jacobian/orbit certificate, and homogeneous
invariant dimensions $1,2,7,14,72$ at field degrees $4,6,8,10,12$.
In the declared derivative-free analytic chiral four-form model, stress flows
are polynomial vector fields on interaction coefficients. Their nonlinear
reachable sets differ from their linear spans: the degree-eight free-seed orbit
is four-dimensional inside a ten-dimensional interaction space and obeys the
nonlinear constraint $b_2+16a^3=0$. Six specified additional homogeneous
invariant generators complete that truncated model. Independent rational
arithmetic checks extend the reduced-model orbit calculation through degrees
ten and twelve, with dimensions six and ten, and construct explicit signed
control sequences. Tensor-to-model certification, reduced-model algebra,
and physical admissibility are separate obligations. This draft reports the
first two only at the verification levels recorded with the executable artifact;
it does not claim an all-orders orbit classification or new physical admissibility
results. Priority and the relation to existing chiral-form work remain under review.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and relation to existing work}
A chiral four-form potential in ten dimensions has a five-form field strength.
The associated auxiliary self-dual five-form permits derivative-free
self-interactions expressed through Lorentz scalar contractions.
The large invariant space makes a precise reachability question possible:
\emph{which interactions are generated from the free seed by polynomial
scalars of the interacting stress tensor, and which additional invariant
controls enlarge that reachable set?}

The existence of 81 functionally independent invariants and the need for
structures beyond stress-tensor scalars in ten dimensions are prior results
\cite{HLS,CHKL}. Graph-based discovery of tensor invariants is also established
\cite{EFS}. The contribution investigated here is a constructive and certified
connection between an explicit atlas, nonlinear coefficient-space obstructions,
and signed-control reachability. Combining software alone is not a novelty
claim. A publication requires comparison of the individual theorem statements
with the cited literature and review of the stress convention.

\section{Representation, invariant atlas, and evidence}
Fix the Lorentz metric $\eta=\mathrm{diag}(-,+,\ldots,+)$ and an orientation for
which the middle-form Hodge operator satisfies $*^2=1$. Let $F=*F$ be real.
The self-dual subspace has $\binom{10}{5}/2=126$ independent components,
parameterized by the increasing index tuples containing the time index.
Each contraction graph has one five-valent vertex per copy of $F$; every edge
contracts one pair of indices with the inverse metric. Slots follow increasing
neighbor order. These choices are part of the formula, not implementation details
to be changed without checking signs.

\begin{proposition}[Independence certificate]
Suppose 81 invariant contractions have an $81\times81$ Jacobian minor that
is nonzero at an integral specialization modulo a good prime. If the generic
Lorentz orbit has dimension 45, then those contractions are algebraically
independent and the invariant rational-function field has transcendence degree
$126-45=81$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
A nonzero modular determinant is a nonzero specialization of the corresponding
integer determinant polynomial. The characteristic-zero Jacobian criterion
therefore gives algebraic independence. The generic orbit dimension supplies
the matching upper bound on the quotient dimension.
\end{proof}

The bundled standalone verifier reconstructs the forms, all 81 values, the full
$81\times126$ Jacobian, 45 infinitesimal Lorentz directions, and integer minors.
The fresh run at $p=50021$ gives Jacobian minor residue 21539 and orbit minor
residue 10547. It checks that the Jacobian annihilates the orbit directions.
A separate tensor engine reproduces all 81 values with identical metric, Hodge,
coordinate and graph conventions.

\section{Interaction space and allowed controls}
Write the auxiliary interaction as a polynomial jet
\begin{equation}
 V=\sum_{d\leq N}\sum_\alpha c_{d,\alpha}I_{d,\alpha}(F),\qquad d=4,6,8,\ldots,
\end{equation}
where each degree has a complete homogeneous basis, including lower products.
The $c_{d,\alpha}$ are independent coefficients in this linear space of
polynomial interactions. They are not values of the invariant functions at a
particular field configuration.

Use the declared HLS normalization $\tau=48T$ and projected auxiliary-field
derivatives. For each allowed trace monomial $g(\tau)$, extracting coefficients
in the invariant basis defines a vector field $X_g(c)$. A control path satisfies
\begin{equation}
 \dot c=\sum_g u_g(\lambda)X_g(c),\qquad c(0)=0.
\end{equation}
Controls are independent, piecewise signed scalar functions. This assumption
is essential: reachability statements below do not impose positivity of
amplitudes, causality, unitarity, or a physical experimental control mechanism.
The free-seed orbit is the set of endpoints of these paths.

If $q(c)=0$ defines an obstruction and $X_g(q)$ belongs to its defining ideal
for every generator, that ideal is invariant. Finding linear spans of vector
field coefficients alone does not identify the nonlinear orbit.

\section{The explicit degree-eight obstruction}
Let $(a,j,k,b_1,\ldots,b_6,p)$ multiply
$(I_4,I_{6,1},I_{6,2},I_{8,1},\ldots,I_{8,6},I_4^2)$.
The seven nonzero truncated control fields are specified by
\begin{align*}
 X_{\tr\tau}&=(20a,40j,40k,60b_1,\ldots,60b_6,60p),\\
 X_{\tr\tau^2}&=2\partial_a+16a^2\partial_{b_1}-96a^2\partial_{b_2}-8a^2\partial_p,\\
 X_{\tr\tau^3}&=\tfrac{32}{3}\partial_j+12a\partial_p,\qquad
 X_{\tr\tau^4}=\partial_{b_1},\\
 X_{(\tr\tau)^2}&=400a^2\partial_p,\qquad
 X_{(\tr\tau)(\tr\tau^2)}=40a\partial_p,\qquad
 X_{(\tr\tau^2)^2}=4\partial_p.
\end{align*}

\begin{theorem}[Degree-eight orbit in the declared reduced model]
The free-seed orbit is the smooth four-dimensional graph
\begin{equation}\label{eq:graph8}
 k=b_3=b_4=b_5=b_6=0,\qquad b_2=-16a^3.
\end{equation}
Its minimal linear invariant hull is five-dimensional.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
For $\Omega_8=b_2+16a^3$, direct differentiation gives
$X_{\tr\tau}\Omega_8=60\Omega_8$ and $X_g\Omega_8=0$ for the other six
fields. The five remaining constraints are homogeneously transported.
They vanish at the free seed, proving necessity.

For arbitrary $(a,j,b_1,p)=(A,J,B,P)$, apply the four controls
\begin{equation}
 \tr\tau^2:\frac A2,\quad
 \tr\tau^3:\frac{3J}{32},\quad
 \tr\tau^4:B-\frac{8A^3}{3},\quad
 (\tr\tau^2)^2:\frac{P+4A^3/3-9AJ/8}{4}.
\end{equation}
Exact integration gives the requested endpoint on \eqref{eq:graph8}, proving
sufficiency. The nonlinear graph allows $b_2$ as an additional linear-span
direction even though it is fixed by $a$ on the orbit.
\end{proof}

For a concrete excluded interaction, set $a=0$ and $b_2=1$, with every other
coefficient zero. It violates $\Omega_8=0$ and is unreachable by pure-stress
controls in this class. Adding the scalar generator $I_{8,2}$ translates $b_2$
and reaches this target. This demonstrates the physical meaning of the
obstruction within the auxiliary interaction model.

\begin{proposition}[Catalogue-scoped completion]
The six additional generators
\[I_{6,2},I_{8,2},I_{8,3},I_{8,4},I_{8,5},I_{8,6}\]
complete the degree-eight
interaction space. Every one is necessary within this specified catalogue.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Through degree eight their new actions are the six constant translations in
those coefficient directions. Combining them with the four constructive
stress controls gives a nonzero constant ten-control determinant, of magnitude
$256/3$. Omitting each extra preserves its corresponding zero coefficient,
or $\Omega_8=0$ when $I_{8,2}$ is omitted. No proper subset of this catalogue
therefore fills the ten-dimensional space. This is not a global minimum over
arbitrary homogeneous or inhomogeneous additional scalar generators.
\end{proof}

\section{Degree ten and degree twelve}
The degree-ten space has 24 cumulative coefficients. The six degree-eight
relations and twelve additional independent relations leave a six-parameter
graph with free coordinates
\begin{equation}
 (A,B,C,D,U,V)=(c_{I_4},c_{I_{6,1}},c_{I_{8,1}},c_{I_4^2},c_{I_{10,1}},c_{I_4I_{6,1}}).
\end{equation}
The artifact gives the twelve relations and verifies exact tangency under every
loaded rational vector field. Four sequential stress controls reach $A,B,C,D$;
the independent constant actions of $\tr\tau^2\tr\tau^3$ and $\tr\tau^5$
then set $U,V$. These calculations classify the declared finite model.
Identifying that model with the tensor physics is a separate map proof.

At degree twelve, restriction to the degree-ten orbit leaves six base and 72
new coordinates. Use the 81-function obstruction ansatz comprising those
72 coordinates and
\begin{equation}
 A^5,A^3B,A^2C,A^2D,AB^2,AU,AV,BC,BD.
\end{equation}
The 36 exact source equations form a matrix $E$ of rank 13. Its nullspace has
dimension 68, with leading coordinate rank 68. The artifact checks $EK^t=0$
exactly for the $68\times81$ constraint matrix $K$ and verifies the triangular
polynomial ideal.

\begin{theorem}[Degree-twelve reduced-model reachability]
Assuming the declared degree-ten map and the 36 degree-twelve source identities,
the free-seed component is a ten-dimensional graph with a six-dimensional base
and four-dimensional affine fibers. Every rational point on that graph is
reached by an explicit ten-stage signed-control sequence.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The 68 independent affine constraints give fiber dimension $72-68=4$.
The controls $(\tr\tau^2)^3$, $(\tr\tau^2)(\tr\tau^4)$,
$(\tr\tau^3)^2$, and $\tr\tau^6$ preserve the base and act by constant
translations on each fiber. On the four free fiber coordinates, the independent
checker reconstructs the exact rational matrix
\begin{equation}
 \begin{pmatrix}
 8&0&0&r_0\\0&0&1024/9&r_1\\0&2&0&r_4\\0&0&0&r_{67}
 \end{pmatrix},\qquad \det=-\frac{16384}{9}r_{67}\ne0.
\end{equation}
All four rational $r_i$, including the nonzero $r_{67}$, are explicitly
recorded in the independent verification JSON. First reach the base with the
six degree-ten stages, tracking the induced fiber displacement. Invert the
four-by-four translation matrix to correct that displacement. Exact
terminating Lie series compute each segment, and every endpoint is checked
against all 68 constraints.
\end{proof}

\subsection{Generalized completion through degree twelve}
For the frozen catalogue of 78 additional homogeneous primitive invariants,
the independent checker constructs the leading constant blocks over $\QQ$.
Their dimensions are $1,2,7,14,72$, and each square determinant is nonzero.
The control family permits products $f(\tau,S)$, not merely independent linear
translations by the extras. Set coefficients in increasing field degree:
solve the constant block at the current degree, then proceed upward. A control
whose leading field degree is larger preserves every coefficient already set.
Under the declared analytic degree filtration, these triangular polynomial
flows give a global finite-jet construction. The exact matrices and selected
generators are included in the generalized-completion report. This establishes
sufficiency for that catalogue; no minimum cardinality among arbitrary nonlinear
controls or scalar choices is inferred.

\section{Physics-map certification and reproducibility}
An exact reduced-model calculation proves a statement about its input
polynomials. It does not, by itself, prove that the polynomials were correctly
derived from the stress tensor. The combined verification separates:
\begin{enumerate}
\item fresh character and tensor independence checks;
\item equality of graph definitions, Hodge conventions and values between two engines;
\item bounded-residual tensor-to-basis map checks;
\item independent exact coefficient-space algebra and constructive endpoints;
\item falsification tests that corrupt signs, generators, source tails and kernels.
\end{enumerate}

For the complete degree-twelve source-map proof, choose 72 fixed small integer
self-dual tensors. Every basis contraction of degree twelve is bounded by
$10^{30}$. A constrained derivative denominator divides $2\cdot5!=240$.
A stress entry contains at most two such derivatives, so a product of $k$
entries is cleared by $(240^2)^k$, together with the denominator of the exact
lower-orbit substitution. This avoids assuming integrality of the full map.
A nonnegative coefficient envelope bounds each cleared source value.
Enough distinct good primes make each bounded integer lift unique.

The run reconstructs the integer evaluation matrix and all 36 cleared source
vectors at the same 72 tensors. A rank-72 minor and the independent homogeneous
dimension bound make evaluation injective. Exact agreement of every candidate
source with its reconstructed values then proves the identities in the declared
invariant space. The computation is resumable and input-hash bound. A run that
has not completed all gates supplies no affirmative map certificate.

The primary commands are:
\begin{quote}\ttfamily
make combined-verify\\
make combined-fresh\\
make combined-full
\end{quote}
They distinguish saved arithmetic, fresh empirical checks, and complete bounded
map computations. Raw logs, environments, source locks, and explicit verification
levels accompany the outputs. Imported historical summaries remain identifiable;
the unified science-status document is the entry point for current claims.

\section{Scope, limitations, and next research questions}
The invariant atlas supports local quotient coordinates and finite homogeneous
bases; a global polynomial-ring presentation and complete syzygy ideal are not
established. Finite-order reachability is distinct from formal orbit equality.
A degree-filtration argument may lift a verified finite obstruction ideal to
an invariant cylinder in the formal theory; it does not prove that all points
of that infinite-dimensional cylinder are reachable.

Nonanalytic interactions such as square-root ModMax potentials require a
separate zero-field analysis. Signed-control reachability does not impose
causality, stability, unitarity, or supersymmetric completion. The auxiliary
interaction convention and its physical interpretation require specialist
review. No inference about Type-IIB completion follows solely from the form
field and spacetime dimension.

The next research questions are an intrinsic formulation under homogeneous
basis changes, minimality of generalized completion under broader scalar choices,
and structural control of the higher-order obstruction tower. Merely adding
higher-degree rank tables would not settle those questions. This draft is
an editable research synthesis, not a claim of journal acceptance or novelty.
AI assistance was used in integration, verification software, and preparation
of this draft; the author must review the mathematical derivations and scientific
interpretation before submission.
